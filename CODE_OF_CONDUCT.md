# Code of conduct

StadiaRef is maintained by Seguru Digital and built with its users. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project, is expected to keep it a place where people can do good work together.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific and useful.
- Accept that people have different experience, and help newcomers.
- Credit others for their work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Email [hello@seguru.digital](mailto:hello@seguru.digital) with what happened and links where you can. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainers will look into every report. They may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may ban someone from the project, temporarily or for good, depending on what happened.

This code of conduct is based on the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
